🩺 Health & Medical Information

Your safety and wellbeing are important to us. Please answer honestly and accurately.

This information helps your tattoo artist identify health, medication, allergy or skin-related considerations that may affect the tattooing process or healing.

If you have concerns about whether tattooing is appropriate for you, please seek advice from an appropriate healthcare professional.

1. Do you have any medical condition, allergy, or health concern that you believe may affect getting a tattoo?

2. Do you have any known allergies or sensitivities to inks, pigments, latex, adhesives, antiseptics, or skincare products?

3. Do you have a condition that may affect healing or bleeding, such as a bleeding disorder, diabetes, or a weakened immune system?

4. Are you currently taking any medication or treatment that may affect bleeding, healing, immunity, or skin sensitivity?

5. Do you currently have any skin irritation, infection, rash, open wound, or other skin condition in the area you would like tattooed?

6. Are you pregnant, breastfeeding, or currently receiving medical treatment that may affect your decision to receive a tattoo?
