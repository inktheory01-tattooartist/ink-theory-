Ink Theory Terms & Conditions

Booking Terms & Client Agreement

1. Appointment Requests

Submitting the online booking form is an appointment request only. It does not automatically guarantee or confirm an appointment. Ink Theory will review the information provided and communicate with the client regarding availability, design requirements and the next steps.

2. Consultation & Pricing

Tattoo pricing may vary according to design complexity, size, placement, session time, detail and other project requirements. Final pricing will be discussed with the client before the appointment is confirmed.

3. Booking / Down Payment

Where a down payment is required, the down payment is used to confirm and reserve the appointment after the booking details have been discussed and accepted. Specific payment instructions will be provided directly by Ink Theory.

4. Rescheduling & Cancellation

Clients should communicate as early as possible if they need to change or cancel an appointment. Rescheduling, cancellation and down-payment policies may depend on the circumstances and will be communicated before the appointment is finalized.

5. Design Changes

Ink Theory works with clients to develop their tattoo concept. Significant design changes requested after artwork has been prepared may require additional time and may affect the final price or appointment schedule.

6. Health & Safety

Clients are responsible for providing accurate and complete health information relevant to receiving a tattoo. Ink Theory may request additional information or postpone an appointment where a disclosed health circumstance could make tattooing inappropriate or unsafe.

7. Client Responsibility

Clients are responsible for following professional aftercare instructions provided after their tattoo appointment. Healing results can vary between individuals and Ink Theory cannot guarantee identical healing outcomes for every client.

8. Age & Identification

Clients must meet all applicable legal age and identification requirements for tattoo services in the location where the appointment takes place. Ink Theory may request valid identification where appropriate.

9. Communication

By submitting the booking form, the client agrees that Ink Theory may use the provided contact details to communicate regarding the requested tattoo, appointment, consultation and related booking matters.

10. Portfolio & Promotional Use

Tattoo artwork may be photographed or recorded for portfolio or promotional purposes where appropriate. Ink Theory will make reasonable efforts to respect client privacy and personal preferences.

11. Intellectual Property

Tattoo artwork and original designs created by Ink Theory remain the intellectual property of the artist unless otherwise agreed in writing. Clients receive permission for the tattoo to be applied to their body but do not automatically receive ownership of the original artwork.

12. Privacy

Information submitted through the booking form is used for appointment administration, communication, client safety and related business purposes. Ink Theory will take reasonable steps to protect submitted information.

13. Acceptance

By submitting a booking request, the client confirms that the information supplied is accurate, understands the nature and risks associated with tattooing, and agrees to these Terms & Conditions.

14. Questions

If you have questions about an appointment, design, payment, health concern or these Terms & Conditions, please contact Ink Theory before submitting or confirming the appointment.
